## Widget Interaction

1. **Docking**: Drag the spectrogram title bar to dock it to different edges
   - Bottom (default) - Full width below video
   - Right - Vertical display beside video
   - Float - Independent window

2. **Visibility**: Toggle with View menu or Ctrl+S

3. **Real-time Updates**: Automatically refreshes every 50ms

4. **Performance**: If compute time > 50ms, frames are dropped (non-blocking)
